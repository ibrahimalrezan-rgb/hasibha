🗂️ About Umm al-Qura Calendar and Date Conversion

Difference Between Hijri and Gregorian Calendars

The Hijri calendar is a lunar calendar based on the moon's monthly cycle, consisting of 12 lunar months totaling 354 or 355 days. The Gregorian calendar is a solar calendar with 365 or 366 days. This ~11 day annual difference causes Islamic months to shift across the seasons approximately every 33 years.
