Guide to the Saudi Citizen Account Program 2026

The Citizen's Account program is a national cash transfer initiative in Saudi Arabia designed to shield citizens from the direct and indirect impacts of economic reforms. It ensures support is targeted efficiently to deserving households.

Allowance Structure:

  • Head of Family: 720 SAR base support.
  • Dependent (Age 18+): 360 SAR (50% of head).
  • Dependent (Under 18): 216 SAR (30% of head).

Payment Schedule:

Payments are deposited monthly on the 10th of every Gregorian month (shifted if falling on a weekend).
