🗂️ The Comprehensive Guide to Personal Finance in Saudi Arabia 2026

What is Personal Finance?

Personal finance is a contract between the customer and the bank under which he grants a sum of money to be used for his personal purposes (marriage, travel, education, treatment, buying a car), in return for paying it in monthly installments that include the original amount plus a profit margin.

Annual Percentage (Apr)

Annual percentage includes profit margin + all administrative fees + insurance cost + any additional expenses. The Saudi Central Bank requires all banks to disclose the Apr clearly.

💡 Interesting piece of information. The nominal percentage may be 3.9% but the actual Apr is 6.2%. Always ask: “What is the Apr including all fees?”

Salary Deduction Percentage

  • Less than 25 Comfortable and secure financial situation.
  • Between 25% and 33%: Acceptable, but keep an eye on your budget.
  • Between 33% and 50%: High and may cause financial stress.
  • 50+ Rejected by most banks.
⚠️ CAUTION: Individuals whose premiums exceed 35% of their income are 3 times more likely to default.

Personal Finances

  • Administration Fees: 1% of the finance amount or SAR 5,000 whichever is less.
  • Early repayment charges Some banks impose it.
  • Late Fee: Penalty for non-payment of instalment on time.

Golden tips

  • Only receive the amount you really need.
  • Read the entire contract before signing.
  • Avoid multiple finances at the same time.
  • Plan for early repayment if you get the chance.
📝 Example: Financing 100,000 riyals, Apr 7%, duration of 5 years = a monthly installment of approximately 1,980 riyals.
