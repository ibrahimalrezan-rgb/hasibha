🗂️ Personal Loan Guide - Saudi Arabia 2026

What is a Personal Loan?

A contract between client and bank granting money for personal purposes, repaid in fixed monthly installments.

APR – The True Cost

APR includes profit margin + all fees. SAMA requires clear disclosure. Always compare APR!

💡 Always ask: "What is the APR including all fees?"

Debt-to-Income Ratio

  • Below 25%: Safe.
  • 25-33%: Acceptable.
  • Above 35%: Financial stress risk.
⚠️ Ratios above 35% are 3x more likely to default.

Golden Tips

  • Only borrow what you need.
  • Read the entire contract.
  • Avoid multiple loans.
