Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Hasibha. Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Hasibha does not collect any personal information directly. We do not require registration and do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal data.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Analytics: We collect anonymous statistical data about site visitors (number of visitors, most visited pages, visit duration) to improve user experience.
  • Google AdSense: We display ads through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests and previous visits. You can disable personalized ads through your Google account ad settings.

Cookies

We use cookies to improve browsing experience and collect statistical data. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect some website functions.

Calculators and Tools

All calculators and tools on Hasibha operate within your browser. We do not store or send the data you enter into the calculators to any external server. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

Information Security

We are committed to protecting your information. We use appropriate security measures to protect our website from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the modified policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
