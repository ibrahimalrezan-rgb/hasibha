Calories and Daily Needs 🗂️ Guide

What are calories?

Calorie is the unit of energy the body gets from food. Your body needs a certain number of calories per day to perform its basic functions and daily activities.

BMR

It is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest. Calculated using the globally approved Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

Your Total Daily Needs (TDEE)

is BMR multiplied by the activity coefficient. These are the calories you need daily to maintain your current weight.

How to slim down and gain weight

  • For slimming: Subtract 500-1000 kcal from TDEE daily to lose 0.5-1 kg weekly.
  • To increase: Add 500 calories over TDEE daily to increase 0.5 kg weekly.
  • To maintain: Consume the same TDEE.
💡 Advice: Do not drop below 1200 calories per day for women or 1500 for men without medical supervision.

Ref: World Health Organization (WHO.int) | Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa)
