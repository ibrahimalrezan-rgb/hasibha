🗂️ Calorie & Daily Needs Guide

What are Calories?

A calorie is a unit of energy your body gets from food. Your body needs a certain number of calories daily for basic functions and daily activities.

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

The number of calories your body burns at complete rest, calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

BMR multiplied by activity factor. This is the calories you need daily to maintain your current weight.

Weight Loss & Gain

  • To lose: Subtract 500-1000 calories from TDEE daily to lose 0.5-1 kg weekly.
  • To gain: Add 500 calories above TDEE daily to gain 0.5 kg weekly.
  • To maintain: Consume TDEE.
💡 Tip: Don't go below 1200 calories for women or 1500 for men without medical supervision.

Reference: World Health Organization (WHO.int) | Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa)
