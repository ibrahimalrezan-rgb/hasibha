🗂️ Guide to Developed Social Security in Saudi Arabia 2026

What is Developed Social Security?

The Developed Social Security System in Saudi Arabia is designed to support the most vulnerable individuals and families, providing targeted financial aid and career empowerment programs to transition beneficiaries toward financial independence.

Standard Monthly Allocations:

  • Head of Family: 1,320 SAR per month.
  • Dependent: 660 SAR per month per qualified member.
  • Food Subsidy: 84 SAR per month per individual.
  • Electricity Subsidy: 72 - 120 SAR per family depending on household size.

How is Calculated Income Evaluated?

Calculated Income = (50% of monthly earned wages) + (100% of unearned income). The benefit equals the maximum allowance minus the calculated household income.
