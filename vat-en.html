Comprehensive 🗂️ Guide to Value Added Tax in Saudi Arabia

What is VAT?

VAT is an indirect tax levied on all goods and services. It was applied in Saudi Arabia 2018 by 5%, then raised to 15% on July 1, 2020. Managed by ZATCA.

How to add tax

Tax Value = Amount x 0.15

Amount after tax = Amount x 1.15

📝 Example: SAR 1,000 + tax = SAR 1,150

How to remove tax

Amount before tax = Amount ÷ 1.15

📝 Example: SAR 1,150 including tax = SAR 1,000 before tax
💡 Advice: To quickly find the pre-tax price, divide by 1.15.

Official References: Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA.gov.sa) | Ministry of Finance (mof.gov.sa)
