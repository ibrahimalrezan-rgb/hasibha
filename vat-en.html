🗂️ Complete Guide to VAT in Saudi Arabia

What is Value Added Tax (VAT)?

VAT is an indirect tax imposed on all goods and services bought and sold in Saudi Arabia. It was introduced on 1 January 2018 at 5% and increased to 15% on 1 July 2020. The tax is administered by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA).

How to Calculate 15% VAT

➕ Adding VAT

To add VAT to an amount (excluding VAT):

VAT Amount = Amount × 0.15

Total Amount = Amount × 1.15

📝 Example: Amount 1,000 SAR
VAT = 1,000 × 0.15 = 150 SAR
Total = 1,000 × 1.15 = 1,150 SAR

➖ Removing VAT

To remove VAT from an amount (including VAT):

Amount Before VAT = Amount ÷ 1.15

VAT Amount = Amount - Amount Before VAT

📝 Example: Amount 1,150 SAR including VAT
Before VAT = 1,150 ÷ 1.15 = 1,000 SAR
VAT = 1,150 - 1,000 = 150 SAR
💡 Quick Tip: To find the price before VAT quickly, divide the total by 1.15. To add VAT, multiply by 1.15.
