Zakat Calculator: A smart tool to calculate the zakat on your money accurately and easily

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam, and performing it accurately and sincerely achieves social solidarity and purifies money. In the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, individuals and investors are keen to calculate their financial zakat with utmost precision in accordance with the provisions of Islamic Sharia. Given the multiplicity and diversity of financial assets, including bank accounts, gold, stocks, and trade offers, some may face difficulty in determining the correct zakat base. Hence the importance of the “Zakat Calculator” as a reliable digital tool that simplifies this complex process, and ensures that you pay the amount of Zakat due with complete legal accuracy and in just a few steps, giving you reassurance and peace of mind in performing this great duty.

Explanation of the Zakat calculator and how it works

The Zakat Calculator is designed to be a comprehensive tool and conform to the jurisprudential rulings approved in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The calculator calculates Zakat based on the prescribed legal percentage, which is (2.5%) for the Hijri year or (2.577%) for the Gregorian year. The calculator relies on your financial inputs to determine whether your money has reached the “shariah quorum” (equivalent to the value of 85 grams of pure gold) and one year has passed (one full Hijri year). The calculator includes all direct zakat assets, such as cash in current and investment accounts, savings, the value of gold and silver (whether bullion or jewelry for savings), and stocks designated for speculation, in addition to the market value of goods and commodities prepared for trade. The calculator also provides the option of deducting the debts and short-term financial obligations owed to you immediately, in order to finally arrive at the actual net Zakat base and determine the amount that must be paid with extreme accuracy.

How to use the zakat calculator

The calculator is easy to use with a simple interface, and you can calculate your zakat by following the following steps:

  • Select the type of calendar used to calculate the year (Hijri or Gregorian).
  • Enter the total amounts of cash and liquidity available in your bank accounts.
  • Enter the value or weight of gold and silver owned for the purpose of saving (in Saudi Riyals).
  • Add market cap of stocks, portfolios and trade offers.
  • Enter the value of your debts and obligations that are due to be paid immediately.
  • Click on the “Calculate” button to display the total Zakat base and the amount of Zakat that must be paid immediately.

Important facts and tips for paying zakat

Legal advice: The zakat quorum is calculated based on the current gold prices in the Saudi market at the time of zakat payment. If your total wealth is less than the value of 85 grams of gold, you do not have to pay zakat.

When paying your zakat in the Kingdom, it is preferable to document payments through approved official payment platforms such as the “Ehsan” platform or the “Zakat” platform affiliated with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA), as these platforms ensure that your zakat reaches its actual recipients in accordance with Sharia and legal controls.

Frequently asked questions

What is a zakat calculator and how does it work?

The Zakat Calculator is an electronic tool that helps you calculate the amount of Zakat you are required to accurately, by entering the total of your money and savings. The calculator automatically calculates the legal amount of Zakat, which is 2.5%, after reaching the quorum and one year around.

How does the Zakat Calculator calculate the Nisab of money?

The calculator compares the total money entered with the value of the legal quorum, which is equivalent to the value of 85 grams of pure gold (24 karat) at the current price. If the money reaches or exceeds this limit, zakat is due on it.

Can a zakat calculator be used to calculate zakat on gold and stocks?

Yes, the Zakat Calculator provides multiple options for calculating Zakat on money, gold, silver, stocks and cash, in addition to trade offers, based on the Sharia controls for each asset.

Are debts and receivables deducted when using the Zakat calculator?

Yes, you can deduct the debts that you must pay immediately from your total funds before entering the net amount in the calculator, and the debts you hope to collect from others are also added.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Update the quorum price daily: Since the price of gold is variable, make sure to use a calculator that uses the real-time price of the Saudi gold market to ensure accuracy.
  • Separate zakat funds from other funds: Do not confuse your personal funds with assets designated for daily use. The basic principle is that zakat is only due on growing wealth.
  • Keep an annual record: Document your assets and debts at the end of each Hijri year, to facilitate calculations and avoid forgetfulness or error.

Frequently asked questions about Zakat Calculator

Is the electronic zakat calculator legally approved?
Yes, the calculators issued by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority are accredited and rely on precise Sharia controls.

Is zakat obligatory on the monthly salary?
Zakat is not obligatory on the salary immediately upon receipt. Rather, it is obligatory on what remains of it after a full year has passed and the quorum has been reached.

What is the ruling on zakat on stocks in the Saudi market?
If the shares are for trading, they are subject to zakat on their market value. As for long-term investment shares, zakat is paid at a rate of 2.5% of their value or profits according to the investor’s diligence.
