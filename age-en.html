🗂️ Age Calculation Guide

Why does Hijri age differ from Gregorian?

The Hijri year is shorter than the Gregorian by about 11 days (354.36 vs 365.25 days). Therefore, Hijri age is greater, and the difference increases with age.

How are zodiac signs determined?

Zodiac signs are based on Gregorian birthdate. There are 12 signs: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces.

Life Stages

  • Childhood: 0-12 years
  • Adolescence: 13-17 years
  • Late Adolescence: 18-21 years
  • Early Adulthood: 22-35 years
  • Late Adulthood: 36-50 years
  • Middle Age: 51-65 years
  • Senior: 66+ years
💡 Note: Calculations are based on Saudi Arabia timezone (Asia/Riyadh).
