🗂️ Guide to calculating age and the differences between the two calendars

Why is the Hijri age different from the Gregorian age?

The Hijri year is shorter than the Gregorian year by about 11 days (354.36 days compared to 365.25 days). Therefore, the age in the Hijri year is greater than the age in the Gregorian calendar, and the difference increases as age increases.

How do you calculate zodiac signs?

The zodiac sign depends on the Gregorian date of birth. There are 12 signs: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces.

Age Calculator 2026: Your comprehensive guide to accurately calculating your age in Saudi Arabia

In light of the digital transformation that the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is witnessing within Vision 2030, electronic tools have become an essential part of our daily lives. And it counts Age calculator It is one of the most requested tools among Saudi users, whether to calculate age in the Hijri or Gregorian calendar, or to find out the retirement age according to the social insurance system, or to verify eligibility in government support programs such as the citizen account. Accuracy in determining age is no longer a luxury, but rather a necessity that affects the interests of both citizens and residents in official and financial transactions.

How does the age calculator work step by step?

The age calculator relies on an accurate mathematical equation that converts the date of birth into the number of days, then distributes it over the remaining years, months, weeks, and days, taking into account leap years that contain 366 days. To make the most of the tool in the Saudi market, follow these steps:

  1. Enter date of birth: Enter the day, month and year as recorded in the national ID or family registry.
  2. Calendar selection: Determine whether you want the result in Gregorian or Hijri, as some Saudi authorities, such as the courts and the Ministry of Justice, officially approve the Hijri date.
  3. Determine today's date: Most calculators allow the current date to be automatically kept or adjusted to calculate age at a future date for planning purposes.
  4. Click on the calculate button: Within seconds you get your age in years, months and days, with additional details such as the day of the week you were born.
  5. Read the composite results: Some advanced calculators also show age in both Hijri and Gregorian terms, and the number of days remaining until your next birthday.

It should be noted that the accuracy of the calculator depends on the accuracy of the entered data, especially when converting between the Hijri and Gregorian calendars, as the annual difference between them is about 11 days.

A practical example from the Saudi market

Suppose that a Saudi employee was born on Shaaban 15, 1410 AH, which corresponds to March 13, 1990 AD. When you enter this date into the age calculator as January 1, 2026, the result will be: 35 years, 9 months and 19 days In AD, equivalent to Approximately 37 Hijri years. Since the regular retirement age in Saudi Arabia is 60 Gregorian years, he has about 24 years and 3 months remaining to complete the period of subscription to social insurance, which gives him an opportunity to plan his retirement salary early.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Check the Hijri date in official contracts: When signing employment or rental contracts, make sure that the age calculated in the Hijri calendar matches what is registered in civil status, to avoid any legal dispute before the judicial authorities.
  • Use the calculator for zakat purposes: Reaching the quorum and the two years of the year are linked to the Hijri calendar, and the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority adopts the Hijri year in calculating zakat on money, so be sure to know your Hijri age accurately.
  • Monitor early retirement age: According to the Social Security System, the earlier you start contributing, the higher your retirement pension. Use the calculator to determine the remaining years and plan your retirement savings.

Frequently asked questions

Is the age calculator officially approved in Saudi Arabia?
It is not a legally approved tool in itself, but it gives accurate results that match the official accounts, and the final reliance remains on official records in civil status.

What is the difference between Gregorian and Hijri age?
The Hijri year is about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year, so the Hijri year is about one to two years older than the Gregorian year, depending on the date of birth.

Does the age calculator affect my eligibility for government support?
Yes, programs such as the Citizen’s Account and the value-added tax on the first residence depend on specific age standards, so check your age carefully before applying.
