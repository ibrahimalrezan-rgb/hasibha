🗂️ Calculating the age and differences between the two calendars

Why is the Hijri age different from the Gregorian?

The Hijri year is about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian (354.36 days vs. 365.25 days). Therefore, the age in Hijri is greater than the age in Gregorian, and the difference increases as you age.

How do you calculate constellations?

The tower depends on the Gregorian date of birth. There are 12 constellations: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Lion, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces.
