🗂️ Comprehensive Guide to Real Estate Finance in Saudi Arabia 2026

What is Real Estate Finance?

Real estate finance is a financial product offered by banks and financing companies to help individuals purchase a home or property. The bank pays the full value of the property to the seller, and then the client repays the amount in monthly installments extending over years, with an agreed-upon profit margin added.

According to statistics from the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), the volume of residential real estate finance in the Kingdom exceeded 600 billion SAR by the end of 2025.

Types of Real Estate Finance

  • Subsidized Finance: Provided by the Ministry of Housing in cooperation with banks, where the state covers a portion of the profit.
  • Murabaha Finance: The bank buys the property and then sells it to the client at a higher price.
  • Ijarah Finance: The bank buys the property and leases it to the client until the end of the term.
  • Commercial Finance: For investors in commercial real estate.

Down Payment

According to the Saudi Central Bank: First home 10% minimum, second home 30%.

📝 Example: A property worth 1 million SAR, down payment 10% = 100,000 SAR paid by you, and the bank finances 900,000 SAR.

Golden Tips

  • Compare 3 banks before signing.
  • Ask about APR, not the nominal rate.
  • Ensure the installment does not exceed 30% of your income.
  • Use the calculator to test different scenarios.

References: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA.gov.sa) | Ministry of Housing (housing.gov.sa) | Real Estate Development Fund (redf.gov.sa)
