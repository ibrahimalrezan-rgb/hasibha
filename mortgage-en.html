🗂️ Comprehensive guide to real estate financing in Saudi Arabia 2026

What is real estate financing?

Real estate financing is a financial product offered by banks and finance companies to help individuals purchase a home or property. The bank pays the full value of the property to the seller, then the customer pays the amount in monthly installments extending over years, adding an agreed-upon profit margin.

According to statistics from the Central Bank of Saudi Arabia (SAMA), the volume of residential real estate financing in the Kingdom exceeded 600 billion riyals by the end of 2025.

Types of real estate financing

  • Supported financing: It is provided by the Ministry of Housing in cooperation with banks, and the state bears a portion of the profits.
  • Murabaha financing: The bank buys the property and then sells it to the customer at a higher price.
  • Ijarah financing: The bank buys the property and rents it to the customer until the end of the term.
  • Trade Finance: For investors in commercial real estate.

Down payment

According to the Central Bank of Saudi Arabia: the first residence is a minimum of 10%, the second residence is 30%.

📝 example: A property worth one million riyals, a down payment of 10% = 100,000 riyals that you pay, and the bank finances 900,000 riyals.

Golden tips

  • Compare 3 banks before signing.
  • Ask for the APR, not the face rate.
  • The premium does not exceed 30% of your income.
  • Use the calculator to try different scenarios.

Real estate financing calculator in Saudi Arabia 2026: Your smart guide to owning the home of your dreams

With the acceleration of the pace of residential ownership in the Kingdom, it is among the targets Saudi Vision 2030, I became Real estate financing calculator An indispensable tool for every citizen thinking about buying a house or land. It gives you a realistic picture of the monthly installment, the total amount paid, and the percentage of deduction from the salary, before entering into any financial commitment. In light of the multiple financing bodies, such as banks and real estate financing funds, the calculator helps you compare and make a decision based on accurate numbers, not guesswork.

How does the step-by-step mortgage calculator work?

The calculator relies on a set of basic inputs that it defines Central Bank of Saudi Arabia Among the real estate financing controls, the most prominent of which are: the value of the property, the down payment, the financing term, and the interest rate (Murabaha). Here are the steps:

  1. Enter the property value: Whether it is ready, under construction, or residential land.
  2. Select the down payment: It is usually not less than 15% of the property value according to current regulations.
  3. Choose financing term: It ranges between 5 and 25 years, with the possibility of extension up to 30 years for some entities.
  4. Determine the Murabaha Rate: It varies between banks and often ranges between 4% and 6% annually.
  5. Add monthly income: To calculate the deduction percentage, which must not exceed 65% of the salary, according to: Social insurance system.

After entering this data, the result will appear to you immediately: the monthly installment, the total amount paid, and the total interest. Some calculators also take fees into account Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority Represented by the real estate transaction tax of 5% upon sale, and in-kind registration fees.

A practical example with numbers from the Saudi market

Let's say you want to buy a villa 1,200,000 riyals In Riyadh, I paid a down payment of 20%, or 240,000 riyals, leaving 960,000 riyals remaining as financing for a period of 25 years with a Murabaha rate of 5.5%. The approximate monthly installment will be approx 5,890 riyalsThe total amount paid over the years amounts to approximately 1,767,000 riyals. If your salary is 12,000 riyals, the deduction rate is 49%, which is within the permissible limit.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Compare funding agencies: Do not rely on one offer, as differences in profit rates may save you thousands of riyals.
  • Calculate additional costs: Valuation fees, property insurance, and property registration should be included in your budget.
  • Consider your other obligations: according to Work system And insurances, your total deductions must not exceed 65% of your salary to avoid default.

Frequently asked questions

Can I obtain real estate financing while I am working in the private sector?
Yes, provided that you are registered with Social Insurance and have a fixed income not less than the limit determined by the financing body, with a period of service often not less than 3 months.

What is the real estate transaction tax and does it affect financing?
It is a 5% tax imposed by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority on the sale of real estate. It is calculated upon sale and not upon purchase, but it must be taken into account when planning for resale.

Is it possible to pay off real estate financing early without penalties?
Yes, according to the controls of the Central Bank of Saudi Arabia, you have the right to early payment, and some authorities may apply nominal fees to the remaining amount, so review the contract before signing.
