🗂️ Comprehensive Guide to Real Estate Financing in Saudi Arabia 2026

What is Real Estate Financing?

Real estate financing is a financial product provided by banks and financing companies to help individuals purchase a home or property. The bank pays the full value of the property to the seller, and the customer then repays the amount in monthly installments extending over years, with an agreed-upon profit margin added.

According to statistics from the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), the volume of residential real estate financing in the Kingdom exceeded 600 billion SAR by the end of 2025.

Types of Real Estate Financing

  • Subsidized Financing: Provided by the Ministry of Housing in cooperation with banks, where the state bears a portion of the profits.
  • Murabaha Financing: The bank purchases the property and then sells it to the customer at a higher price.
  • Ijarah Financing: The bank purchases the property and leases it to the customer until the end of the term.
  • Commercial Financing: For investors in commercial real estate.

Down Payment

According to the Saudi Central Bank: first home 10% minimum, second home 30%.

📝 Example: A property worth 1 million SAR, down payment 10% = 100,000 SAR paid by you, and the bank finances 900,000 SAR.

Golden Tips

  • Compare 3 banks before signing.
  • Ask about the APR, not the nominal rate.
  • The installment should not exceed 30% of your income.
  • Use the calculator to test different scenarios.

References: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA.gov.sa) | Ministry of Housing (housing.gov.sa) | Real Estate Development Fund (redf.gov.sa)
