🗂️ Mortgage Financing Guide - Saudi Arabia 2026

What is Mortgage Financing?

Mortgage financing helps citizens and residents own their dream home without paying the full property value upfront. SAMA reports the residential mortgage portfolio exceeded 600 billion SAR by end of 2025.

Types of Mortgage Financing

  • Subsidized Mortgage: Ministry of Housing with banks.
  • Islamic Murabaha: Shariah-compliant.
  • Ijarah (Lease-to-Own): Bank leases then transfers ownership.
  • Commercial Mortgage: For investment properties.

Down Payment

First home: 10% minimum. Second home: 30% minimum.

💡 Golden Tip: Higher down payment = lower monthly payments and less total interest.

Golden Tips Before Signing

  • Compare offers from at least 3 banks.
  • Ask about all fees.
  • Don't exceed 30% of your income.
