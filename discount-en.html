🗂️ Guide to calculating discounts and discounts

How do you calculate the price after the discount?

Price after discount = original price - (original price x discount percentage ÷ 100)

📝 example: The price of 200 riyals with a 25% discount = 200 - (200 x 25 ÷ 100) = 200 - 50 = 150 riyals

How do you calculate the value of the savings?

Saving value = original price x discount percentage ÷ 100

Tips when purchasing at discounts

  • Compare the price after the discount with the price in other stores.
  • Make sure the original price is real and not exaggerated.
  • Don't buy just because of the discount if you don't need the item.
💡 advice: 50% off something you really need is an excellent saving.

Discount percentage calculator in Saudi Arabia 2026: Your smart guide to saving money intelligently

With the acceleration of the pace of electronic shopping in the Kingdom, and the increase in the volume of e-commerce to more than 200 billion Saudi riyals in accordance with the goals of Vision 2030, the Saudi consumer is faced with daily offers and discounts that do not stop. But did you know that many offers appear tempting on the surface while hiding a much lower real discount? Here the importance arises Discount percentage calculator As an indispensable tool for every shopper who wants to know the real final price before paying, whether in physical stores or electronic platforms such as Noon, Amazon Saudi Arabia, and Jarir. In this article, we provide you with a comprehensive guide to using the calculator intelligently in the Saudi market in 2026.

How does the discount percentage calculator work?

The discount percentage calculator is based on a simple but effective mathematical equation: Final price = original price x (1 - discount percentage ÷ 100). To calculate the actual saving value in riyals, we subtract the final price from the original price. You can follow the following steps:

  1. Enter Original price For the item in Saudi Riyals as it appears on the price card or product page.
  2. Enter Discount percentage The declared rate, whether it is 15%, 30%, 50%, or any other percentage.
  3. Click the account button to appear Price after discount directly.
  4. verify Value added tax The 15% rate applied in the Kingdom according to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, as the prices displayed may or may not include tax.
  5. Compare the result with Shipping cost If the purchase is electronic, to ensure that the discount is actually deserved.

It should be noted that some Saudi stores offer the discount as a percentage on the original price, while others offer it as a fixed price after the discount, so the calculator helps you extract the real percentage in both cases. Knowing the final price also helps you plan your monthly budget, especially with obligations such as social insurance premiums and Saudi Labor Law salaries that determine your disposable income.

A practical example from the Saudi market

Suppose you want to buy an iPhone from a Saudi online store, and its original price 4,500 riyals, and therefore a percentage discount 25% On the occasion of National Day. Using the calculator: Discount value = 4,500 x 25 ÷ 100 = 1,125 riyalsAnd the price after discount = 4,500 - 1,125 = 3,375 riyals. Adding 15% VAT to the discounted price, the total becomes 3,881.25 riyals. Notice how the final price is higher than the discounted price by SAR 506.25, which is a difference that should be taken into account before making a purchasing decision.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Check original price before discount: Some stores raise the price and then announce a fake discount, so compare the price across more than one reliable Saudi platform.
  • Calculate tax and shipping: Add 15% VAT and shipping cost to find out the real cost, and do not rely solely on the displayed price.
  • Take advantage of the seasons: Seasons such as Ramadan, National Day, and Foundation Day offer discounts of up to 70%, so plan your purchases in advance to achieve maximum savings.

Frequently asked questions

Do the prices shown in Saudi Arabia include VAT?
According to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, stores must disclose prices including the 15% tax to the final consumer, but some electronic offers may display the price before tax, so always check the details.

Can the calculator be used to calculate salary or end-of-service deductions?
Yes, the same ratio principle applies, but calculating the end of service is subject to the Saudi labor system and has special equations that depend on the length of service and the type of contract termination, and it is preferable to refer to the official platforms.

What is the best way to ensure that the advertised discount is correct?
Use the discount percentage calculator to compare the final price with the original price, and follow product prices for at least two weeks before purchasing, especially in high commercial seasons.
