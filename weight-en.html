What is a Weight Converter?

Hasibha's weight converter helps you convert any value between different weight and mass units accurately.

Supported Units (17 Units)

  • Metric: Kilogram, Gram, Milligram, Microgram, Metric Ton.
  • Imperial: Pound, Ounce, US Ton, UK Ton, Stone, Dram, Hundredweight, Grain.
  • Arabic Units: Mithqal, Oqqa, Mann.
  • Other: Carat.
📝 Example: 1 kg = 1000 g = 2.2 lb = 35.27 oz
💡 Tip: 1 Mithqal = 4.25 grams approximately.
