🗂️ Guide to drinking water and daily needs

Why is water important?

Water makes up 60% of the human body and plays a vital role in regulating temperature, transporting nutrients, expelling toxins, and moisturizing the joints.

How to calculate daily needs

General rule: 35-55 ml of water per kilogram of weight depending on activity. The more active you are, the more water you need.

Signs of dehydration

  • Extreme thirst
  • Dark colored urine
  • Headache
  • Dizziness
  • Dry mouth and skin
💡 advice: Drink water regularly throughout the day, and do not wait until you feel thirsty.

Reference: Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa) | World Health Organization (WHO.int)

How does the step-by-step water need calculator work?

The calculator is based on an approved scientific equation that combines several individual and environmental factors, and you can use it easily through the following steps:

  1. Basic data entry: Weight in kilograms, height in centimeters, age, and gender, as men usually need 10% to 15% more fluids than women.
  2. Determine the level of physical activity: From sedentary (office work) to very active (field work or sports), each level adds an additional 300 to 800 milliliters.
  3. Choosing climatic factors: Temperature and humidity in your city. If the temperature exceeds 40 degrees, the need increases by about 500 milliliters per day.
  4. Determine health status: Pregnancy, breastfeeding, or chronic diseases such as diabetes and kidney disease that may require medical adjustment.
  5. Read the result: The calculator shows your daily requirement in liters and cups, with a suggested distribution over the hours of the day.

The basic equation depends on multiplying the weight by a minimum of 30 milliliters, then adding the activity and climate factors. This trend is linked to the occupational health programs encouraged by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development as part of the quality of life initiatives in the work environment. It also integrates with health prevention efforts supported by the competent authorities to raise the productivity of the Saudi employee.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Distribute drinking throughout the day: Drink a cup every hour instead of large quantities at once, especially between the dawn and evening prayers in the summer months.
  • Monitor the color of urine: A light yellow color is a good indicator of hydration, while a dark color indicates dehydration and requires an immediate increase in fluids.
  • Beware of soft drinks and excessive coffee: It is a diuretic and may increase fluid loss. Replace it with water or water with lemon and mint added.

Frequently asked questions about the water requirement calculator

Does the result differ between men and women?
Yes, women usually need 10% to 15% less fluids due to differences in muscle mass and hormones.

Can pregnant and breastfeeding women rely on the calculator?
They can be used as a general reference, but it is recommended to increase between 300 and 700 milliliters per day and consult a doctor to determine the exact number.

Do medications or chronic diseases affect the outcome?
Yes, some blood pressure and diabetes medications and diuretics change the body’s needs, so you must consult a doctor before relying completely on the calculator.
