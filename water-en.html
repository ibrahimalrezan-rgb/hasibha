🗂️ Guide to drinking water and daily needs

Why is water important?

Water makes up 60% of the human body and plays a vital role in regulating temperature, transporting nutrients, flushing out toxins, and moisturizing joints.

Method of calculating the daily requirement

General rule: 35-55 ml of water per kg of weight by activity. The more active you are, the more water you will need.
