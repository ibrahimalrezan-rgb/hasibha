🗂️ Water Intake Guide

Why is Water Important?

Water makes up 60% of the human body and plays a vital role in regulating temperature, transporting nutrients, removing toxins, and lubricating joints.

How to Calculate Daily Water Needs

General rule: 35-55 ml of water per kilogram of body weight depending on activity. The more active you are, the more water you need.

Signs of Dehydration

  • Extreme thirst
  • Dark urine color
  • Headache
  • Dizziness
  • Dry mouth and skin
💡 Tip: Drink water regularly throughout the day, not just when you feel thirsty.

Reference: Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa) | World Health Organization (WHO.int)
