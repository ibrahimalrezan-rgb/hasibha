True Landed Cost Economics for Saudi Importers

Successful importing into Saudi Arabia requires looking far beyond the supplier's initial FOB factory invoice. Total landed cost incorporates customs duties, Saber platform compliance, port handling, shipping insurance, and 15% import VAT.

Landed Cost Structure

  1. Ex-Factory Goods Value: Base purchase price from supplier.
  2. International Ocean / Air Freight: Transport costs to Saudi entry ports (Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port Dammam, Riyadh Dry Port, or King Khalid International Airport).
  3. ZATCA Customs Duties: Applied on CIF value (typically 5% for standard consumer goods).
  4. Import VAT (15%): Assessed on total (CIF Value + Customs Duty).
  5. Regulatory & Clearance Fees: Saber conformity certificate, Fasah platform clearance, port handling, and inland transport to warehouse.
