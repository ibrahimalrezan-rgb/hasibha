Retail Pricing Strategy in KSA: Margin vs Markup

Product pricing represents the single most influential lever on retail business health. The mathematical difference between gross margin and cost markup causes significant confusion among new founders:

  • Markup: Profit expressed as a percentage of unit cost.
  • Margin: Profit expressed as a percentage of selling price.

Break-Even Volume Formula

Break-Even Units = Total Monthly Fixed Overhead ÷ Unit Contribution Margin
