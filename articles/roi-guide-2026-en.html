Evaluating Investment Feasibility with ROI

Return on Investment (ROI) measures the efficiency of capital deployed across entrepreneurial ventures, retail stores, or e-commerce brands in Saudi Arabia.

Core Financial Formulas

  • Annual ROI (%) = (Annual Net Profit ÷ Invested Capital) × 100
  • Payback Period (Months) = Invested Capital ÷ Monthly Net Profit
