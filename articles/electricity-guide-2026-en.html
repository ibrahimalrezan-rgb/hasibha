Understanding the Saudi Residential Electricity Tariff

Residential electricity in Saudi Arabia operates on a tiered billing structure managed by the Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) under regulatory oversight by the Water and Electricity Regulatory Authority.

Residential Consumption Brackets 2026

Tier Bracket Monthly Consumption (kWh) Rate per kWh
Tier 1 (Subsidized)1 - 6,000 kWhSAR 0.18 / kWh
Tier 2Above 6,000 kWhSAR 0.30 / kWh

Bill Breakdown & VAT

The total monthly invoice includes:

  • Energy consumption cost across tiers.
  • Meter service charge based on circuit breaker capacity (typically SAR 10 - SAR 30).
  • 15% Value Added Tax (VAT) applied to the subtotal.
