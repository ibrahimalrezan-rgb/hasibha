What is an Area Converter?

Hasibha's area converter helps you convert any value between different area units accurately for real estate, agriculture, and engineering.

Supported Units (12 Units)

  • Metric: Square Meter, Square Kilometer, Square Centimeter, Square Millimeter, Hectare, Are.
  • Imperial: Square Mile, Square Yard, Square Foot, Square Inch.
  • Arabic Units: Acre, Dunam.
📝 Example: 1 Hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.47 Acres = 10 Dunams
💡 Tip: In Saudi Arabia, 1 Dunam = 1,000 m².
