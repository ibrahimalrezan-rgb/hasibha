How does the area conversion calculator work?

The area conversion calculator relies on fixed mathematical equations linking different units, and works according to the following steps:

  1. Input unit selection: The user specifies the unit they have, such as square meter, square foot, hectare, dunum, or karat.
  2. Enter numeric value: Write the number to be converted in the designated field, making sure to include decimal separators.
  3. Output unit selection: Specifies the target unit, such as converting from square feet to square meters when comparing old real estate deeds to modern units.
  4. Application of constant coefficient: The calculator multiplies the value by the adopted factor, for example 1 square meter = 10.7639 square feet, and1 hectare = 10,000 square metres, and1 dunam = 1,000 square metres.
  5. Show result: The converted value appears immediately and can be rounded to decimal places as needed.

The calculator adheres to the units approved in General Authority for Survey and Geospatial InformationIt is the official reference for measuring areas in the Kingdom, which ensures the accuracy of the results when used in documented real estate transactions.
