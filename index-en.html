Frequently Asked Questions

Are Hasibha calculators free?

Yes, all calculators are 100% free with no registration or subscription required, and will remain so forever.

Is my data saved or sent to any third party?

No. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser (Client-Side) and no data is sent to or stored on our servers.

Are the calculators designed for the Saudi market?

Yes, financial calculators are designed according to Saudi regulations: financing rates and terms, GOSI social insurance deductions, and end of service benefits calculation.

How is mortgage payment calculated?

We use the standard loan payment formula considering the deduction ratio and financing term approved in the Kingdom.
