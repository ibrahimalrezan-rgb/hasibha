💡 Saudi Electricity Tariffs & Consumption Guidelines 2026

1. Official SEC Tariff Slabs:

  • Residential Tier 1 (1 – 6,000 kWh): 0.18 SAR per kWh.
  • Residential Tier 2 (>6,000 kWh): 0.30 SAR per kWh.
  • Commercial Tier 1 (1 – 6,000 kWh): 0.20 SAR per kWh.

2. Summer Energy Conservation Tips:

Air conditioning accounts for roughly 70% of residential power bills during summer. Setting AC units to 24°C (75°F) and cleaning air filters monthly can lower power consumption significantly to remain within the 18 halala slab.
