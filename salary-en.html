🗂️ Guide to social insurance discounts in Saudi Arabia

What is social insurance?

A mandatory system administered by the General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) that provides social protection for Saudi employees in the public and private sectors.

Old system (9%)

9% deduction from salary for insurance only.

📝 Salary of 10,000 riyals: discount = 900 riyals, net = 9,100 riyals

New system (11%)

  • 9% insurances
  • 2% SANED (breakdown compensation)
📝 Salary of 10,000 riyals: discount = 1,100 riyals, net = 8,900 riyals

The difference between the two systems

The new system adds 2% to the Sanid system, which provides compensation of 60% of the salary for a period of 12 months in the event of unemployment.

💡 advice: The discount applies only to Saudis. Non-Saudis are subject to a 2% system borne by the employer.

Official references: General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI.gov.sa) | Sanad system Ministry of Human Resources (HRSD.gov.sa)

Salary calculator after insurance in Saudi Arabia 2026: Your comprehensive guide to knowing your net salary

Knowing the net salary after deducting social insurance and other deductions is one of the most important priorities for every employee in the Saudi market, whether he is a citizen or resident. With the continuous updates to social insurance and value-added tax systems, it has become necessary to use Salary calculator after insurance To get an accurate picture of your actual income. In this article, we provide you with a comprehensive practical guide that explains how the calculator works, with realistic examples and advice to help you plan smartly financially in accordance with the Kingdom’s regulations for the year 2026.

How does the salary calculator work after insurance?

The calculator depends on a set of basic inputs determined by the Social Insurance System and the Saudi Labor System, the most prominent of which are:

  • Basic salary: The amount agreed upon in the contract without allowances.
  • Housing allowance: It is calculated within the contribution base up to a maximum of three times the basic salary.
  • Other allowances: Such as transportation and transportation allowance, and is subject to subscription according to the insurance regulations.
  • Saudi employee subscription rate: 9.75% of the wage subject to contribution (including the pensions branch, 9% and the occupational hazards branch, 0.75%).
  • Resident subscription rate: Only 2% of the wage subject to contribution (Occupational Hazards Branch).

Simple steps to use the calculator:

  1. Enter basic salary, housing allowance and other allowances in the designated fields.
  2. Specify nationality (Saudi or non-Saudi) because percentages vary.
  3. Add any additional deductions such as advances or private medical insurance if applicable.
  4. Click on the calculate button to show you the subscription value and the deduction percentage.
  5. View your net salary after insurance, ready for comparison with your salary statement.

The calculator depends on updates General Organization for Social Insurance For the year 2026, taking into account the maximum contribution wage of 45,000 Saudi riyals per month. The calculator also takes into account that some allowances, such as education allowance or overtime allowance, are not subject to contribution.

A practical example from the Saudi market

Suppose that a Saudi employee works in a private company with a basic salary of 10,000 riyals, a housing allowance of 2,500 riyals, and a transportation allowance of 500 riyals. Contribution wage = 13,000 riyals. The employee percentage is calculated as 9.75% to equal 1,267.50 riyals. Therefore, net salary = 13,000 − 1,267.50 = 11,732.50 riyals. However, if he is a resident employee with the same salary, his contribution rate is only 2%, or 260 riyals, and his net salary is 12,740 riyals. This fundamental difference explains the importance of determining nationality when using the calculator.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Check your monthly payslip: Ensure that the deduction matches the official percentages and avoid accounting errors from the employer.
  • Keep a record of your insurance contributions: Check your balance through the “My Insurance” application to ensure that your period of service is calculated correctly upon retirement.
  • Plan your budget on net, not gross: Rely on your salary after insurance to determine your monthly obligations, especially with the rise in living costs in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is the housing allowance subject to insurance contributions?
Yes, the housing allowance is subject to subscription provided that it does not exceed three times the basic salary, according to the social insurance regulations effective in 2026.

Can a Saudi employee reduce his contribution rate?
No, the percentages are set by law at 9.75% for a Saudi employee, and the employer is committed to 11.75%, and it is not permissible to agree otherwise.

Does the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority impose a tax on the salary?
There is no income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia, but the Authority imposes a value-added tax on goods and services, and it may indirectly affect the purchasing power of the net salary.
