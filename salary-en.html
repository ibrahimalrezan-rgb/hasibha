🗂️ Complete Guide to GOSI Deductions in Saudi Arabia

What is GOSI?

The General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) is a mandatory system in Saudi Arabia that provides social protection for Saudi employees in both public and private sectors through monthly salary deductions.

Old System (9%)

The old system deducts 9% of the employee's gross salary:

  • 9%: Employee's contribution to social insurance (pension branch).
📝 Example: Salary 10,000 SAR
Deduction = 10,000 × 9% = 900 SAR
Net = 10,000 - 900 = 9,100 SAR

New System (11%)

The new system deducts 11% of the employee's gross salary:

  • 9%: Employee's contribution to social insurance (pension branch).
  • 2%: SANED (unemployment insurance).
📝 Example: Salary 10,000 SAR
Deduction = 10,000 × 11% = 1,100 SAR
Net = 10,000 - 1,100 = 8,900 SAR

Difference Between the Two Systems

  • Old System: 9% deduction for GOSI only.
  • New System: 11% deduction (9% GOSI + 2% SANED).
  • SANED provides 60% of salary for up to 12 months in case of unemployment.
💡 Note: Deductions apply only to Saudi employees. Non-Saudi workers are subject to 2% GOSI (occupational hazards branch) paid by the employer.

Official References: General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI.gov.sa) | SANED System | Ministry of Human Resources (HRSD.gov.sa)
