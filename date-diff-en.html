🗂️ Guide to calculating the difference between two dates

How do you calculate the difference between two dates?

Enter the start date and end date. The calculator calculates the difference in years, months, days, weeks, hours and minutes.

📝 example: From 01-01-2024 to 01-01-2025 = 1 year = 12 months = 365 days

When do you need this calculator?

  • Calculating the length of job service.
  • Calculating the duration of pregnancy.
  • Calculating the duration between two occasions.
  • Calculating the duration of subscriptions.
  • Calculate the warranty period.
💡 advice: Make sure you enter both dates correctly. The order does not matter because the calculator calculates the absolute difference.

Time calculator between two dates in Saudi Arabia 2026: Your comprehensive guide to accurately calculating time periods

In light of the rapid digital transformation that the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is witnessing within Vision 2030, the time calculator between two dates has become an indispensable tool for every citizen and resident. It serves the employee who needs to calculate his length of service in the social insurance system, the investor who follows tax grace periods with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, and the employer who adheres to the requirements of the Saudi labor system in calculating leaves and end-of-service benefits. With the increase in digital contracts and legal dates, accuracy in calculating the days, months and years between two dates has become vital to avoid fines and document rights.

How does the time between two dates calculator work?

The calculator relies on an accurate algorithm that compares the start date and the end date, then extracts the difference in years, months, days, and even hours and minutes when needed. The steps for use are summarized as follows:

  1. Enter start date: Specify the day, month and year in Gregorian or Hijri format according to the required system, taking into account that some Saudi systems, such as social insurance, rely on the Gregorian calendar in subscription calculations.
  2. Enter end date: Enter a target date, whether it's a contract expiration date, a tax due date, or the day a certificate is issued.
  3. Choose the unit of measurement: Some calculators allow you to specify whether you want the output in days only, months, years, or a combination of them.
  4. Taking into account leap years: The calculator automatically adjusts the number of days in February in leap years such as 2024 and 2028, eliminating full-day errors.
  5. Exclude or include start day: Some tools allow you to determine whether the starting day is counted within the period, which is essential in calculating the notice period in the Saudi labor system.
  6. View detailed result: The result appears in cumulative format, such as “2 years, 3 months, 12 days,” and can be copied or exported.

This calculator is used in various fields in the Saudi market, including calculating the employee’s length of service to be eligible for the end-of-service reward in accordance with Article 84 of the Labor Law, calculating the regular notice period of 30 or 60 days, and determining the remaining period for submitting the value-added tax return before the fifteenth day of the following month.

A practical example from the Saudi market

Suppose that a Saudi employee began his work in a technology company in Riyadh on March 1, 2022, and his contract ended on June 15, 2026. When these two dates are entered into the calculator, the result is: 4 years, 3 months and 14 days. Since the period of service exceeded five years when the notice period was added, the employee is entitled to a full end-of-service reward according to the work system, that is, half a month’s wages for each year of the first five, and a full month’s wages for every subsequent year. The calculator also allows the employer to verify the period of subscription to social insurance, which is approximately 51 months, which is a sufficient period to be entitled to SANED compensation in the event of unemployment.

Three important tips for the Saudi user

  • Take into account the difference between the Hijri and Gregorian calendars: The Hijri year is approximately 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year, which affects vacation calculations and insurance contributions, so make sure you choose the correct calendar.
  • Keep a documented record of the dates: Save contracts, invoices and official notices, because the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority may request proof during a tax audit.
  • Check for updated systems: Amendments may occur to the labor system or insurance regulations, so check official sources before final approval of any account.

Frequently asked questions about the time between two dates calculator

Q1: Does the calculator calculate official holidays and public holidays?
No, the calculator only calculates the calendar time period, and does not exclude official holidays such as Founding Day or National Day, unless you use the “Business Days” option in some advanced tools.

Q2: Can it be used to calculate the notice period in the Saudi labor system?
Yes, as the system stipulates a notice of no less than 30 days for contracts of indefinite duration, and 60 days in some cases, and the calculator can accurately determine the date of the end of the relationship.

Q3: What is the difference between calculating the period in Gregorian and Hijri months in social insurance?
The General Organization for Social Insurance uses the Gregorian calendar when calculating contributions, while some real estate and rental agencies use the Hijri calendar, so the correct calendar must be determined before calculating.
