🗂️ Date Difference Calculation Guide

How to Calculate Difference Between Two Dates

Enter the start date and end date. The calculator shows the difference in years, months, days, weeks, hours, and minutes.

📝 Example: From 2024-01-01 to 2025-01-01 = 1 year = 12 months = 365 days

When to Use This Calculator

  • Calculate employment duration.
  • Calculate pregnancy duration.
  • Calculate time between events.
  • Calculate subscription periods.
  • Calculate warranty periods.
💡 Tip: Make sure to enter both dates correctly. Order doesn't matter because the calculator calculates the absolute difference.
