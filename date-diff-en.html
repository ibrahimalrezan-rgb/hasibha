🗂️ Calculating the Difference Between Two Dates

How do you calculate the difference between two dates?

Enter start date and end date. The calculator calculates the difference in years, months, days, weeks, hours and minutes.

📝 Example: From 2024-01-01 to 2025-01-01 = 1 year = 12 months = 365 days

When do you need this calculator?

  • Calculating the duration of the job service.
  • Calculation of gestation period.
  • Calculate the duration between two occasions.
  • Calculate the duration of subscriptions.
  • Calculation of Warranty Period.
💡 Advice: Make sure both dates are entered correctly. It doesn't matter the order because the calculator calculates the absolute difference.
