In the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, gold is considered a safe investment option and an essential part of culture and savings. With the continuous changes in gold prices globally and locally, it becomes necessary for investors and those buying or selling to know the exact value of their gold holdings clearly and transparently. The “Gold Value Calculator” provides you with a smart and quick tool to estimate the true financial value of gold based on direct data from the Saudi market. Whether you want to calculate the value of investment bullion, ornamental jewelry, or even determine the value of zakat on gold due, this calculator ensures that you get accurate results that help you make sound and informed financial decisions with complete ease.

How does the gold value calculator work?

The “Gold Value Calculator” relies on precise mathematical equations that link three main factors: weight in grams, gold karat, and the current price of a gram of gold in Saudi riyals. Pure gold is measured in 24 karat (99.9% purity) and is the primary standard for determining the direct price on the exchange. When choosing other carats, such as 21 (the most common in Saudi Arabia), 18, or 22, the calculator multiplies the price of a gram of 24 carat by the purity percentage of the specified carat, then multiplies the result by the total weight of the piece.

Furthermore, the calculator provides a fair estimate that reflects the value of pure gold metal (fraction). It should be noted that the value calculated via the digital calculator represents the “price of raw gold,” and does not include the “workmanship fee” added by jewelers, or the applicable value-added tax, which gives you a fair and clear minimum value of your gold when you want to sell or evaluate it before purchasing.

How does the gold value calculator work?

The gold value calculator depends on three basic data: the weight of gold in grams, the karat of gold, and the instantaneous price of a gram in the Saudi market. Here are the detailed steps:

  1. Weight input: The user enters the weight of gold in grams, whether it is jewelry, bullion, or liras.
  2. Determine the caliber: The user chooses the karat (24, 22, 21, 18) which is essential because the price per gram varies with purity.
  3. Instantaneous price per gram: The price is updated automatically from reliable sources such as the global stock exchange and the local gold market in Riyadh and Jeddah.
  4. Calculating the total value: The gram value is multiplied by the weight, then the manufacturing margin is added, if any.
  5. Taxes and fees deduction: According to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, the 15% value-added tax applies to workmanship only, and not to the value of the metal itself.

The calculator is distinguished by its accuracy in converting karats. For example, a gram of 24 karat gold equals 1,000 of purity, while 21 karat equals 0.875, and 18 karat equals 0.750. This conversion ensures you know the true value of the pure metal inside your piece.

A practical example from the Saudi market in 2026

Suppose that the price of a gram of 24 karat gold in Riyadh on 2026 was 320 Saudi riyals. A Saudi woman owns a 21-karat gold bracelet weighing 40 grams, with a manufacturing price of 25 riyals per gram. The account is as follows:

  • The value of a gram of 21 karat = 320 x 0.875 = 280 riyals.
  • Metal value = 280 x 40 = 11,200 riyals.
  • Workmanship = 25 x 40 = 1,000 riyals.
  • Value added tax on workmanship = 1,000 x 15% = 150 riyals.
  • Total value = 12,350 riyals.

When selling, the profit margin, which ranges between 5 and 10 riyals per gram, is deducted, which means that the actual purchase price from the merchant is slightly less than the selling price to the consumer.

Important tips for the Saudi user

  • Check the real time price: Gold prices change daily, so be sure to use a calculator linked to an immediate updating source before any buying or selling.
  • Understand the difference between buying and selling price: The merchant buys from you at a price lower than the announced price, and this difference is called the “spread” and may reach 10 riyals per gram.
  • Keep purchase invoices: The invoice guarantees your rights in the event of claiming zakat or when selling, and it is a basic requirement in commercial transactions according to Saudi regulations.

Frequently asked questions

Does the gold value calculator include zakat?
No, the calculator only calculates the market value. As for Zakat, it is calculated at 2.5% of the value of the gold if it reaches the quorum (equivalent to 85 grams of 24 karat) and one year has passed, according to the provisions of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.

Is processed gold subject to VAT?
Yes, the value-added tax of 15% is applied to manufacturing wages only, while the value of the raw metal is exempt from tax.

Can a calculator be used to calculate used gold?
Yes, but it must be taken into account that the dealer deducts an additional percentage ranging between 3% and 7% due to the condition of the part and re-melting expenses.
