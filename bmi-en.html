BMI 🗂️ Index

What is BMI?

BMI is a measure that determines if your weight is appropriate for your height. It is calculated by dividing the weight in kilograms by the square of the length in meters.

BMI Categories by who

  • Less than 18.5: skinny, scraggy
  • 18.5–24.9 Standard weight
  • 25 - 29.9: Weight gain
  • 30 - 34.9: First class 🟠 obesity
  • 35 - 39.9: Second Degree 🔴 Obesity
  • 40 ⚫ Obesity
📝 Example: A person weighing 70 kg and 175 cm long = 70 ÷ (1.75) ² = 22.86 (normal)
💡 Advice: BMI does not differentiate between muscle and fat. Athletes may appear to be overweight due to muscle mass.

Ref: World Health Organization (WHO.int) | Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa)
