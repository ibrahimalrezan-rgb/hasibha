🗂️ BMI Guide - Body Mass Index

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index measures if your weight is appropriate for your height. It's calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI Categories (WHO)

  • Below 18.5: 🔵 Underweight
  • 18.5 - 24.9: 🟢 Normal weight
  • 25 - 29.9: 🟡 Overweight
  • 30 - 34.9: 🟠 Obesity Class I
  • 35 - 39.9: 🔴 Obesity Class II
  • 40+: ⚫ Obesity Class III
📝 Example: Person weighing 70 kg and 175 cm tall = 70 ÷ (1.75)² = 22.86 (Normal)
💡 Tip: BMI doesn't differentiate between muscle and fat. Athletes may appear overweight due to muscle mass.

Reference: World Health Organization (WHO.int) | Saudi Ministry of Health (moh.gov.sa)
