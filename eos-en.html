🗂️ End of Service Benefit Guide - Saudi Labor Law 2026

What is the End of Service Benefit?

A fundamental financial right under Saudi Labor Law (Royal Decree M/51), calculated on last basic salary, service period, and termination reason.

How is it Calculated?

  • First 5 years: Half month per year.
  • After 5 years: Full month per additional year.
💡 Example: Salary 10,000 SAR, 8 years = 55,000 SAR.

Resignation – Article 85

  • Under 2 years: No benefit.
  • 2-5 years: 1/3 of benefit.
  • 5-10 years: 2/3 of benefit.
  • Over 10 years: Full benefit.

Tips to Protect Your Rights

  • Document your contract on Qiwa.
  • Keep salary slips.
  • Use the calculator above.
