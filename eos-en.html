🗂️ The Comprehensive Guide to End of Service Gratuity in the Saudi Labor Law 2026

What is an end of service gratuity?

The end of service gratuity is a basic financial right guaranteed by the Saudi Labor Law issued by Royal Decree No. (M/51). It is an amount disbursed by the employer to the worker upon the end of the employment relationship, and is calculated based on the last basic salary, the period of service, and the reason for the end of the employment relationship.

How is the bonus calculated?

  • First five ears Half a month (15 days) for each year of service.
  • After five years: A full month (30 days) for each additional year.
  • Fractions of the year: Calculated as a percentage of the full year.
📝 Example: Basic salary 10,000 riyals, service 8 years. First 5 years: 5 x 5,000 = 25,000. Last 3 years: 3 x 10,000 = 30,000. Total = SAR 55,000.

Resignation and its impact – Article (85)

  • Less than two years He is not worthy of any reward.
  • ~2-5 Years Worth a third of the reward.
  • ~5-10 Years Worth two-thirds of the reward.
  • More than 10 years. He deserves the full reward.

Cases in which the full reward is due

  • Expiry of the contract – Article (74).
  • Closure of the facility – Article (74).
  • Reaching retirement age – Article (74).
  • Termination of the contract without a legitimate reason – Article (77).
  • Death or disability of the worker – Article (79).
  • Work injury – Article (137).

Cases where the reward is not worthy

⚠️ Important The worker's right to remuneration shall be forfeited in these cases:
  • Disciplinary dismissal – Article (80).
  • Termination of the contract by the worker without a reason – Article (81).
  • During the probationary period – Article (53).

Tips to ensure your rights

  • Trust your contract to Qiwa.sa.
  • Keep payroll and correspondence.
  • Ensure that the basic salary is clearly stated in the contract.
  • Do not sign a prior resignation or blank paper.
  • Use the calculator above before reviewing HR.

How do you behave if the reward is not disbursed?

  1. Submit an amicable application to the employer in writing.
  2. File a lawsuit in the Wedi platform (moj.gov.sa).
  3. If the settlement is not possible, the case shall be referred to the Labor Court.
