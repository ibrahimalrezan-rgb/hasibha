📋 Saudi Labor Law Annual Leave Regulations 2026

1. Statutory Leave Entitlement (Article 109):

  • Employees are entitled to prepaid annual leave of no less than 21 days per year for service under 5 years.
  • Leave scales to a minimum of 30 days once the employee completes 5 consecutive years of service.
  • Leave wages must be disbursed in advance prior to the start of the leave period.

2. Wage Basis for Vacation Encashment (Article 111):

Under Article 111, upon termination or resignation, employees are entitled to cash encashment for any accrued unused annual leave days calculated on the basis of their last actual wage (Basic + Housing + Transport + Fixed Allowances).
