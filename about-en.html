About Us & Methodology — Hasibha.com

Last Regulatory Review: November 2026

Hasibha — Specialized Saudi Calculation Platform

Hasibha (Hasibha.com) is an independent Saudi digital platform designed to provide accurate, transparent, and user-friendly financial, labor, and regulatory calculators for citizens, residents, and businesses across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our Vision & Mission

We aim to be the most trusted calculation and decision-support reference in Saudi Arabia by offering reliable, instant, and 100% free tools that comply with official regulations without requiring sign-up or storing personal data.

Calculation Methodology & Statutory Sources

All calculators on Hasibha are grounded in published regulatory decrees and verified formulas:

  • End of Service Gratuity: Based on Articles (84) and (85) of the Saudi Labor Law and actual last wage provisions (Article 2) with statutory termination ratios.
  • Mortgage & Personal Loans: Formulated according to the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) Responsible Lending Principles, incorporating maximum debt burden (DTI up to 55%-65%) and full APR disclosure including capped administrative fees.
  • Social Insurance & Salary: Configured with the General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) and SANED contribution schedules under the 2024–2026 updates.
  • Zakat & VAT (15%): Modeled per Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) standards and Sharia nisab thresholds.
  • Health & Wellness: Built on World Health Organization (WHO) and Mifflin-St Jeor metabolic equations.

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Your sensitive financial information never leaves your device. All calculations execute directly in your browser without transmitting numbers or personal data to our servers or third parties.

Editorial & Quality Assurance Team

Our platform is maintained by a dedicated team of software developers and financial content reviewers who routinely audit and update equations following official circulars issued by SAMA, Qiwa, the Ministry of Human Resources, and ZATCA.
