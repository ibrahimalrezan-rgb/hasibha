📚 Essential Guide to GPA & Academic Standings in Saudi Arabia 2026

1. Saudi University Grading Scales (5.0 vs 4.0):

Most public universities in Saudi Arabia (such as King Saud University, Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud, Umm Al-Qura, and King Abdulaziz University) utilize the 5.0 GPA scale, where A+ equals 5.0 points and passing starts at 2.0. KFUPM, Alfaisal, and American curriculum programs utilize the standard 4.0 scale.

2. Saudi High School Track System Weights:

Under the Ministry of Education's High School Track framework, cumulative GPA is weighted as follows: Year 1 Common (20%), Year 2 Specialized (40%), and Year 3 Specialized (40%).

3. First and Second Honor Standing:

  • First Class Honors: Cumulative GPA of 4.75 – 5.00 (or 3.75 – 4.00) without failing any courses.
  • Second Class Honors: Cumulative GPA of 4.25 – 4.74 (or 3.25 – 3.74) without failing any courses.
